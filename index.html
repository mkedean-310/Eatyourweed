The Science

Know Your Cannabinoids

CBD

Cannabidiol

CBD · Non-Psychoactive

The workhorse of wellness. CBD supports calm, reduces discomfort, and promotes balance without any intoxicating effect — making it the most widely embraced cannabinoid for daily nutrition.

CBG

Cannabigerol

CBG · The Mother Cannabinoid

Often called the "stem cell" of cannabinoids — CBG is the precursor from which all others are born. It's gaining attention for its potential to support mood, gut health, and mental clarity.

CBN

Cannabinol

CBN · The Sleep Cannabinoid

As cannabis ages, THC transforms into CBN — a mildly sedative, deeply relaxing compound. Beloved by those seeking natural support for restful sleep and nighttime recovery.

THC

Tetrahydrocannabinol

THC · Where Permitted by Law

The most famous of all. When consumed as food, THC metabolizes into 11-hydroxy-THC — a more potent, longer-lasting experience than smoking, prized for deep therapeutic effect.

Food is
Medicine

Humans have been consuming cannabis as food and medicine for over 10,000 years. We're just rediscovering what our ancestors already knew.

Your body already speaks the language of cannabis. The endocannabinoid system — a vast signaling network running throughout your nervous system, immune response, and gut — is literally designed to interact with plant-based cannabinoids. You were built for this.

"Let food be thy medicine, and medicine be thy food."
— Hippocrates, 400 BC

Eating cannabis — rather than smoking or vaping it — delivers a fundamentally different, often more therapeutic experience. The plant's compounds pass through the digestive system, engage the liver, and enter the bloodstream in a form that can last four to eight hours, providing sustained relief for pain, anxiety, insomnia, and inflammation.

At EatYourWeed.com, we believe in the whole plant. Not isolated extracts. Not synthetic compounds. The entourage effect — the synergy of cannabinoids, terpenes, and flavonoids working together — is where the real magic lives.
